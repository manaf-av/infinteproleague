Infinite Club Register

Get Your Club Recognised by Infinite Pro League

The Infinite Club Register is an initiative to identify and recognise esports and gaming clubs across colleges, universities and communities. Introduce your club, share your experience and help us understand your ambitions for the future of esports.

Why Register?

  • Opportunity for recognition by Infinite Pro League.
  • Consideration for future partnership programs and collaborations.
  • Potential opportunities to participate in competitions and community initiatives.

Tell Us About Your Club

Send us an email introducing your club. Please include:

  • Club Details: Name, location, institution and representative.
  • Current Members: Number of active members and games you focus on.
  • Experience: Events organised, tournaments participated in and achievements.
  • Expectations: What you expect from Infinite Pro League and what opportunities or support you seek.
  • Future Goals: Your plans and ambitions for the club.

There is no fixed registration form. Share your club profile in your own format.

Email:
players@infiniteproleague.com

Subject: Infinite Club Register – [Club Name]

Registration does not guarantee recognition or partnership. All submissions will be reviewed by Infinite Pro League.
